Gatherings made sweeter

A simple plan for
your next get-together.

Birthday, club meeting, or office gathering? We will help you organize dessert around the people coming.

From idea to event.

  1. Share the details. Send your event date, guest count, location, and preferred pickup or delivery time.
  2. Review your plan. We discuss flavors, ingredient concerns, portions, and the total quote.
  3. Confirm the booking. Your event is booked only after we confirm availability and you accept the quote.
  4. Enjoy your gathering. Pick up your prepared boxes or meet us at your agreed delivery location.

A little notice helps.

Please contact us at least 72 hours before your event. For groups over 50, allow one week. Availability is confirmed individually.

Changes and cancellations should be discussed as soon as possible. Any applicable terms will be included in your quote.

Planning your portions

How much should you plan for?

Two cookies per guest is a useful starting point. We can adjust the amount if dessert is the main event or part of a larger meal.

Example serving guide

12 guests 24 cookies 72 hours
24 guests 48 cookies 72 hours
