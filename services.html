Our services

Less planning.
More celebrating.

We handle dessert planning, baking, and local delivery so you can spend more time with your guests.

01 / PLAN

Event dessert planning

We work with your headcount, schedule, and budget to suggest portions and a flavor mix. Every event starts with a conversation.

  • Portion guidance for your group
  • Flavor and ingredient discussion
  • A written quote before confirmation
02 / PREPARE

Baking & packing

Your event batch is baked and packed for sharing. Labeled boxes make it easy to set out a dessert table without extra preparation.

  • Fresh batches for your event
  • Clearly labeled cookie flavors
  • Boxes arranged for easy serving
03 / DELIVER

Local event delivery

We arrange a handoff in Somerville or Medford. Pickup is also available by appointment at the location confirmed with your booking.

  • A delivery window agreed in advance
  • Coordination with your event contact
  • Pickup as an alternative to delivery

A familiar mix of favorites.

Our event selections include Classic Chocolate Chip, Double Dark Chocolate, and Cinnamon Sugar. We help you choose a mix for your gathering during planning.

Our kitchen handles wheat, milk, eggs, soy, and nuts. Tell us about dietary needs before booking so we can discuss ingredients and limitations. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.
